About Desi Markets

Desi Markets helps shoppers explore Indian grocery, festive, hair-care, skincare and personal-care products through organized category catalogs.

What the website does

The website turns product-feed information into simpler browsing experiences with category rows, product imagery, price references and links to partner merchant pages.

Independent discovery

Desi Markets is not the merchant of record. The website focuses on discovery, navigation and clear affiliate disclosure.

Our approach

  • Organize products into practical shopping categories.
  • Use accessible, responsive product carousels.
  • Identify sponsored relationships clearly.
  • Direct visitors to the merchant for current product and transaction details.
