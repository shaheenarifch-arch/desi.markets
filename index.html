FAQ

Before you order

What is Desi Markets?

Desi Markets is an independent guide to trusted South Asian online stores. We don't sell products ourselves. We point you to established stores, starting with Distacart for Indian groceries, sweets, snacks and puja essentials, and we're adding Pakistani and Bangladeshi stores next.

Is Distacart legit and safe to order from?

Distacart is an established online store that ships Indian products from India to 35+ countries. As of September 2026 it is rated 4.5 out of 5 (“Excellent”) on Trustpilot from more than 4,800 reviews, and you pay on its own secure checkout. The most common complaint in reviews is customs delays, so leave extra time before festivals.

Does Distacart ship to the USA, Canada, the UK and Australia?

Yes. Distacart lists express shipping to the USA, the UK, Canada, Australia, the UAE, Singapore and many more countries, over 35 in total.

How long does delivery to the US take?

Distacart says orders usually dispatch within 3–5 business days. US delivery then takes about 3–5 days with Priority shipping or 6–12 days with Express, plus customs clearance. For festivals, we suggest ordering about three weeks ahead.

Will I pay customs duties or import taxes?

Distacart says its order totals include taxes and customs, and it offers credit for extra duties under its “Dista Promise.” Import rules change, so always review the final total at checkout.

Is shipping free?

As of September 2026, Distacart lists free US Express shipping on orders over $59 and free Priority shipping on orders over $120. Thresholds can change, so check Distacart's shipping page before you order.

Do I pay more if I use your links?

No. You pay the same price. If you buy through our links, we may earn a small commission, which keeps this guide free.

When are Pakistani and Bangladeshi stores coming?

We're reviewing stores now and will add them once they meet our standards for range, reliability and secure checkout. You can suggest a store or join our newsletter to hear first.
